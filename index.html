Our Story

135 Years in the Alberta Foothills

Carved into the breathtaking Alberta foothills since 1885, this storied land has hosted frontier legends, Hollywood royalty, and generations of visionaries. Today, that legacy reemerges as Canada's premier private ranch estate — where heritage, elevated design, and uncompromising hospitality converge.

Coming to the Ranch

The Lodge & Event Barn

Designed by Bennett Partners, the architectural vision marries rugged alpine timber with warm interior stonework. Anchored by soaring exposed trusses and towering stone hearths, the Lodge and Event Barn offer an intimate yet majestic backdrop for high-end galas, weddings, and corporate retreats.

  • The Event BarnSoaring vaulted roofs, grand wrought-iron accents, and expansive seating for premier galas and ceremonies.
  • The LodgeAn architectural haven of timber and stone, featuring cozy hearth fires, curated spirits, and breathtaking wraparound mountain decks.
  • The GroundsManicured pond edges, covered stone walkways, and pristine open range framed by the Canadian Rockies.
