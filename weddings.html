One Place, One Weekend, One Guest List

A buyout hands you the gate key. Ceremony in the pasture with the Rockies behind you, dinner under the trusses of the event barn, drinks in the loft until the sky goes properly dark, and breakfast the next morning with only the people you invited.

The Shape of It

A Three-Day Ranch Wedding

Friday

Arrival & Welcome

Guests roll in down the Cowboy Trail. Drinks along the covered walk, a fire in the log house, and an informal supper in the great hall.

Saturday

Ceremony & Dinner

Ceremony on the pasture, cocktails in the horse barn, a long seated dinner in the event barn, dancing until the band gives out.

Sunday

Ranch Morning

Breakfast on the lodge deck, a trail ride for anyone still standing, and a slow goodbye at the gate.

Any Season

Winter Weddings

Snow on the foothills, string lights on the barn, skijoring if you're brave. The ranch does not close in October.
