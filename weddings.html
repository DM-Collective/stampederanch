The Shape of It

A Ranch Wedding Might Look Like…

An illustration of how a weekend can unfold, not a fixed package. Your ranch representative will shape the days, the spaces and the amenities with you.

Friday

Arrival & Welcome

Guests roll in down the Cowboy Trail. Drinks along the covered walk, a fire in the log house, and an informal supper in the great hall.

Saturday

Ceremony & Dinner

Ceremony on the pasture, cocktails as the light goes, a long seated dinner under the trusses, dancing until the band gives out.

Sunday

Ranch Morning

Breakfast on the lodge deck, a trail ride for anyone still standing, and a slow goodbye at the gate.

Any Season

Winter Weddings

Snow on the foothills, string lights on the barn, skijoring if you're brave. The ranch does not close in October.
