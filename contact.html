Good to Know

Frequently Asked

How far is the ranch from Calgary?

About an hour southwest, in Longview, Alberta, on the Cowboy Trail. Most guests drive; groups often arrange a coach from Calgary for evening events.

Can we book a single building rather than the whole ranch?

Yes. Every venue on the Venues & Events page can be booked individually. Full-ranch buyouts are the standard path for weddings, but they're available to any group that wants the place to itself.

What's the largest event the ranch can hold?

The new event barn is designed for seated dinners of roughly three hundred, and the pasture accommodates considerably more under marquee. Final capacities will be confirmed with the ranch team.

Do you host events in winter?

Year-round. Winter is one of the most striking times on the ranch — snow on the foothills, string lights on the barn, and skijoring for anyone up for it.

How do deposits and payments work?

Deposits and balances are handled through secure online payment at checkout, alongside your booking. The exact deposit schedule is confirmed with your booking agreement.

Is the ranch still a working cattle operation?

It is — and it has been since 1885. Guests share the property with a working ranch, which is a large part of why the place feels the way it does.
